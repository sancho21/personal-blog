I’m a Software Technical Lead based in Kuala Lumpur, with over 15 years of experience building and improving software systems across banking, telecommunications, pharmaceuticals, insurance, and enterprise technology.

My expertise is rooted in backend engineering, software architecture, DevOps, and cloud-native systems. I enjoy solving complex technical problems and turning them into solutions that are reliable, scalable, and maintainable.

I’ve worked extensively with Java, Spring Boot, Python, Kubernetes, Docker, and CI/CD, while leading teams, improving engineering practices, and designing systems for critical business environments. One of my notable projects was a high-performance transaction switching system used by more than five major national banks.

I’m also passionate about exploring emerging technologies, particularly AI, automation, and modern software development tools. My recent work includes LLM inference on CPU-only machines and building private AI applications.

Outside of work, I enjoy building side projects, experimenting with technology, playing with microcontroller e.g. Raspi, ESP32, and continuously learning how to build better software.
